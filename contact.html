FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I know which HAIRX shampoo is right for me?
Each colour targets one concern. Take our 60-second hair quiz for a personal match, or message us on WhatsApp and we'll help you choose.
How do I use it?
Apply on damp hair. Massage a coin-sized amount into your scalp for about 60 seconds, leave it for 1–2 minutes, then rinse well. Use it 3–4 times a week, or daily if you sweat a lot.
Why is each shampoo a different colour?
The colours come from natural pigments that are also active ingredients, such as spirulina (blue), beetroot (red), turmeric (amber) and bamboo charcoal (grey). There are no synthetic dyes.
Is it really free from harmful chemicals?
Yes. HAIRX is free from SLS/SLES sulphates, parabens, silicones, mineral oil, phthalates, formaldehyde-releasers and artificial dyes. See our lab reports.
When will I see results?
Most men feel a difference after the first wash: less oil, less itch, softer hair. Visible changes in dandruff usually take about 2 weeks, and hair fall about 6–8 weeks of regular use.
Do you offer Cash on Delivery?
