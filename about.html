Our story

Men were told one shampoo fits all. It never did.

HAIRX started with a simple frustration: the shampoo aisle treats every man's scalp the same. Dandruff, oily roots, dryness and hair fall are different problems, and each one needs its own answer.

The problem

What men's scalps are dealing with

1 in 2

Dandruff

About half of adults deal with dandruff at some point. Men are more prone to it, because more oil feeds the yeast that causes flakes.

More oil

Oily scalp

Men's skin tends to produce more sebum. Add heat, helmets and sweat, and hair goes flat and greasy within hours.

Up to 85%

Hair fall

Up to 85% of men see noticeable thinning by 50. Stress, pollution and harsh shampoos make it worse.

Stripped

Dry scalp

Sulphate-heavy shampoos strip natural oils. The result is tightness, itching and dry flakes that are often mistaken for dandruff.

Humidity

Frizz

Indian monsoons and hard water lift the hair cuticle, so thick and curly hair puffs up and won't stay in place.

Daily

Sweat & buildup

Gym, sport and commuting leave salt, sweat and pollution on the scalp, which can clog follicles and cause odour.
